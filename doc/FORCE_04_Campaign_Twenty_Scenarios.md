# FORCE

## Campaign — twenty scenarios

*Four chapters · three boards · one idea each*

Twenty missions. Four chapters of five. Each chapter adds one idea. Recycle three layouts: Farm, Ridge/River, Town. Skin them with different portals and clocks. Fail states the player can read off the paint: farm gone, portal still closed, convoy cell dead.

### Boards

| Board | Rooms (typical) | Missions that live here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farm | yard, lane, orchard, shed line | 1, 5, 8, 11, 20 and variants |
| Ridge / River | crest, reverse, bank, ferry, causeway | 2, 6, 7, 9, 12, 16 |
| Town / Loop | block, church, road, loop rooms | 3, 4, 10, 13–15, 17–19 |

### Chapter 1 — One room, one verb

One screen. Teach the line, force, and K.

### 1. Hold the farm

Three buildings, one section. Wave 1 infantry on one axis. Wave 2 heavier, same axis. Wave 3 three flanks. Win: farm still yours when the clock ends. Teach D, force, C. A retrieval truck at the end is flavor; M to it.

### 2. Take the ridge

Attack uphill. Default ROE smears across two nests. Teach K on the first, then the second.

### 3. Open the lane

Hedge with one gap. Halt in cover, K the covering gun, A through the gap.

### 4. Night watch

Fog almost full. Contacts late and loud. Teach C / E and do not chase every red cell.

### 5. Rally at the church

Two sections. One already breaking. Teach H, fall back, and J so replacements go to the weak letter.

### Chapter 2 — Doors and tools

Still small or 1.5 screens. Portals and engineers.

### 6. Blow the culvert

Hold until engineers finish destroy on a road cell. Clock versus incoming column.

### 7. The wooden bridge

Closed portal. Engineers build; defend the near bank. Portal opens: one section crosses, one holds.

### 8. Mine the orchard

Lay, then bait. A room can be a weapon.

### 9. Clear the causeway

Clear mines under fire. Force 3 on the clear team, force 8 on overwatch.

### 10. Lost section

A section starts in the wrong room. Path exists only after a farmhouse portal is retaken. Teach the graph without naming it.

### Chapter 3 — Two fronts

2×2 overview matters. Zoom, C, neglect.

### 11. Two farms

Hold both. First time the overview is the game.

### 12. Ridge and river

Take the ridge and keep the ferry. Stare at one, lose the other.

### 13. Patrol the loop

No action expected. Four quiet rooms, then two contacts at once, then a third behind you. No new unit types.

### 14. The fake retreat

Fall back through three rooms. Enemy follows the first two. Third room is a kill sack if you K the road cell, not the crowd.

### 15. Convoy

A slow truck stack must cross. Sections are escort. Chase every contact and the convoy dies in a room you left.

### Chapter 4 — Ugly problems

Same verbs, worse timing. One special toy per mission.

### 16. Mortars on the reverse slope

Cannot K what you cannot see. Eyes on the crest, fire mission from behind. WHERE can be a cell you do not occupy.

### 17. Town block

Three buildings, every room a door. Focus fire or take fire from two windows.

### 18. Counterpunch

Start defending, then a window: leave a force-4 stay-behind, A their battery with the rest. Wait for safe and the window closes.

### 19. Fog and friends

Two sections arrive late off-map. Hold with too little, then J so new meat does not walk into the wrong room.

### 20. The last farm

Mission 1 geography at 2×2 scale. Three axes, one engineer crisis, one nest that must be K’d or the yard dies. Graduation: every verb in ten minutes.

### Design rules for the list

- One teaching target per mission.
- Win/lose readable from overview colors.
- No hidden spreadsheet score. A one-line ticker is enough: A held the farm. C broke at the river.
- If a mission needs more than about eight live orders to win, it is too big for this UI.
